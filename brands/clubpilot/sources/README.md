# Sources: Byron's own words

The material Byron's LinkedIn posts are written from (source mode, `engine/generate/founder.js`). Every
sentence of a founder post restates something in here, with the quote, and the source gate checks it.

What goes here, and only this:

- Voice memos of Byron talking, from his phone (`.m4a`, `.mp3`, `.wav`, under 25 MB): the easiest way
  in. Answer the questions the engine sent, out loud. Drop them in the Drive folder Sources, or upload
  them here. A memo is read as all Byron's words, so a recorded conversation goes in as a transcript.
- Things Byron wrote: posts, notes, emails, answers typed out (Word, Markdown, text).
- Call transcripts with speaker turns (`Byron: ...`), as Zoom or Otter export them. Only Byron's turns
  count; what other people said is context.

Never: an article about Club Pilot, a colleague's notes, or anything written for Byron. Everything in a
file without speaker turns is treated as Byron's own words.

`<file>.transcript.json` beside a memo is its transcript, made once. Correct it by editing the text.
